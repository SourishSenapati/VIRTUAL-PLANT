## 1. Fluid Catalytic Cracking Unit (FCCU / R-201 & R-202)

### 1.1 Riser Reactor & Disengager Vessel (R-201)
- **Riser Geometry:**
  - Diameter: $1,400\ \text{mm}$ ID
  - Vertical Length: $38,000\ \text{mm}$ ($38.0\ \text{m}$)
  - Refractory Lining: $125\ \text{mm}$ Resco Castable RS-17EG hex-mesh refractory with abrasion-resistant erosion tile facing.
- **Feed Injection Nozzles:** 8 multi-orifice steam-atomized oil feed nozzles disposed circumferentially around riser base ($45^\circ$ upward injection angle).
- **Reactor / Stripper Vessel:**
  - Diameter: $4,800\ \text{mm}$ ID
  - Height: $28,000\ \text{mm}$
  - Separation System: Two-stage high-efficiency cyclone separators (4 primary roughing cyclones with direct-coupled diplegs + 4 secondary high-efficiency cyclones).
  - Spent Catalyst Stripper: Two-stage baffles with superheated stripping steam ring ($3.5\ \text{bar(g)}, 250^\circ\text{C}$).

### 1.2 Catalyst Regenerator (R-202)
- **Vessel Architecture (Turbulent Fast Fluidized Bed):**
  - Diameter: $11,500\ \text{mm}$ ID
  - Overall Height: $32,000\ \text{mm}$ ($32.0\ \text{m}$)
  - Operating Temp / Pressure: $690 - 720^\circ\text{C}$ / $2.8\ \text{bar(g)}$
  - Metallurgy: SA-387 Gr 11 Class 2 with internal dual-layer refractory ($100\ \text{mm}$ insulating light refractory + $25\ \text{mm}$ dense abrasion-resistant hex-mesh).
- **Combustion Air Grid:** Domed pipe grid distributor with 360 bubble-cap nozzles delivering $145,000\ \text{Nm}^3/\text{h}$ air from main air blower.
- **Regenerator Cyclones:** 12 pairs of two-stage cyclones with refractory-lined diplegs and flapper trickle valves for continuous catalyst recovery.
- **Catalyst Slide Valves:** Hydraulically actuated refractory-lined slide valves on regenerated and spent catalyst U-bends with emergency automated sealing trips.

## 2. High-Pressure Hydrocracking Unit (HCU / R-301 & R-302)

### 2.1 First-Stage Hydrotreating Reactor (R-301)
- **Operating Conditions:**
  - Operating Pressure: $155.0\ \text{bar(g)}$ ($15.5\ \text{MPa}$)
  - Operating Temperature: $380 - 410^\circ\text{C}$
- **Vessel Dimensions:**
  - Inside Diameter: $3,600\ \text{mm}$
  - Height (Tangent-to-Tangent): $28,000\ \text{mm}$
  - Shell Wall Thickness: $185\ \text{mm}$ heavy-wall forged shell ring construction (SA-336 Gr F22V / $2.25\ \text{Cr} - 1\ \text{Mo} - 0.25\ \text{V}$ Vanadium-modified alloy steel).
  - Internal Cladding: $6.0\ \text{mm}$ weld overlay of TP 347 Stainless Steel.
- **Catalyst Beds:** 3 fixed catalyst beds (NiMo on gamma-alumina) separated by quench mixing boxes with hydrogen quench gas injection distributors.

### 2.2 Second-Stage Hydrocracking Reactor (R-302)
- **Operating Conditions:** $165.0\ \text{bar(g)}$ / $395 - 425^\circ\text{C}$
- **Dimensions:** $3,800\ \text{mm}$ ID $\times 32,000\ \text{mm}$ height, $195\ \text{mm}$ forged wall thickness.
- **Catalyst Beds:** 4 fixed beds of Noble-Metal Pt-Pd on Zeolite/Alumina cracking catalyst for heavy hydrocarbon molecular breakdown and ring saturation.
- **High-Pressure Separator (V-301):** Cold high-pressure separator operating at $150\ \text{bar(g)}$ separating recycle hydrogen gas from liquid hydrocarbon effluent.
